Every song in Gigboy is stored as plain **ChordPro** text, so your songs are never locked into the app: you can always download them as ordinary files.

## The library

The **Library** is every song your band owns. Click a song to open it. The list/grid switch at the top right changes how songs are displayed.

![The band library.](library)

### Search, sort and filter

- Type in the search box to match **title, artist, tags or language**.
- The sort menu orders songs by name, artist, language or date added.
- **Filters** narrows the list by musical key and by tempo (slow, mid, upbeat, fast).

![Filtering by key and tempo.](library-filters)

## Adding songs

You have three ways to get a song in:

- **Write it** — press the round **+** button in the Library and fill in the form (see [Writing a song](#writing-a-song)).
- **Import files** — use the upload button at the top of the Library ("Import song files or a zip backup"). Drop in ChordPro or OnSong files, or a whole `.zip` backup, and Gigboy converts them.
- **Paste a chart** — paste a chord sheet from Ultimate Guitar, Chordify or CifraClub into the lyrics box and Gigboy converts it to ChordPro. Chords written in solfège (Do Re Mi) are recognised too.

> **Tip:** Choose a list under **Save to song list** on the form, or add the song while you are looking at a songlist, to file it straight away.

## Reading a song

![A song: settings, tools and the chord sheet.](song-view)

The song page has three parts:

- **Settings** — change how *you* see the song. These choices stick to the song for you, and each bandmate keeps their own, so the horn player can read in E♭ without touching anyone else's chart.
- **Tools** — [concert mode, tuner, metronome, notes, recorder and more](/help/practice-tools).
- **Actions** — add to a list, update from a file, download, history, edit and delete.

### Transpose, capo and chord names

- The **Key** arrows move every chord up or down a semitone. The key in the middle shows where you are.
- **Capo** shows the chords you play with a capo on that fret.
- **Guitar / Ukulele / Piano** switches which chord diagrams are shown when you tap a chord.
- **C D E / Do Re Mi** switches between letter names and solfège.

## Writing a song

Press the pencil next to the title, or **Edit** on the song page.

![The song form.](song-edit)

Fill in the title, artist, language, key, BPM, time signature, duration and tags. Add a YouTube or Spotify link to get a small player in the song's toolbar.

![The ChordPro editor.](song-edit-lyrics)

The lyrics box uses ChordPro: put chords in square brackets right where they fall in the lyrics, like `[G]Amazing [C]grace`. The buttons above the box help you:

- **Chord** marks the selected text as a chord.
- **Intro, Verse, Chorus, Bridge…** insert section markers.
- **Insert Tab** and **Insert Strum** add a guitar tab or a strumming pattern that can be played back.
- **Preview** shows how the song will look, and undo/redo are always there.

Your work is **autosaved** as you type.

## Edit history

Every save is kept. Open **History** on a song to see who changed what, compare two versions line by line, and restore an older one.

## Deleting and restoring

Deleting a song moves it to the **Trash** in the sidebar. Trashed songs, songlists, setlists and press kits can be restored for 30 days; after that they are gone for good.

## Downloading

**Download** on a song gives you a plain ChordPro file. To take everything at once, see [Your account and your data](/help/account-and-data).
