title: Practice and rehearsal tools
summary: Tuner, metronome, recorder, handwritten notes, attachments and the chord finder.
---

The **Tools** row on every song page puts the things you reach for in rehearsal one tap away. Each tool opens in a panel right on the song; press its **×** to close it.

## Metronome

![The metronome panel.](tool-metronome)

Press play to start the click. It starts at the song's own BPM; change the tempo with **−** and **+** or by tapping it in. The dots show the beat, and the speaker button mutes the click if you only want the visual pulse.

## Tuner

![The tuner panel.](tool-tuner)

Press **Start tuner**, allow microphone access when your browser asks, and play a string.

> **Tip:** On iPhone and iPad the microphone, tuner and recorder only work when your Gigboy is served over HTTPS.

## Recorder

![The recorder panel.](tool-recorder)

Record a take straight from the song page, or **Upload file** to add one you already have. Recordings are saved to the song, so the whole band can listen back.

- Press play on a take to listen.
- Leave a **comment at a moment in the take** ("fix the turnaround at 1:12"). Clicking the comment later jumps the player to that spot.
- Recordings count toward your band's storage, shown in the sidebar.

## Handwritten and typed notes

![The notes panel.](tool-notes)

Tick **Draw** to sketch directly on the song sheet with your finger or a stylus — circle a tricky bar, mark a breath. Tick **Type** to write on the sheet with the keyboard. Your notes are yours; you can also show your bandmates' notes and switch them on and off one author at a time.

## Attachments

Attach PDFs to a song — scanned sheet music, a lyric sheet, a horn chart. Each file can be up to 20 MB. Open **Attachments** to view or download them.

## Chord finder

![The chord finder.](tool-chord-finder)

Look up a chord shape for guitar, ukulele or piano. If your band plays a chord differently from the built-in diagram, pin your own fingering: it replaces the built-in diagram everywhere, including in [concert mode](/help/concert-mode).

## Tabs and strumming patterns

Songs can contain **tabs** and **strumming patterns**. Press **Play** under one to hear it and the **loop** button to repeat it; the same button becomes **Stop** while it plays. Add them from the song editor with **Insert Tab** and **Insert Strum**.
