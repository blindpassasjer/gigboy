Gigboy is a songbook and gig-prep app for your band. Everything you and your bandmates need for a gig — songs, setlists, riders, press kits and the gig calendar — lives in one shared place, and it works on your phone as well as on a laptop.

## Joining a band

There is no public sign-up. Someone invites you:

1. Open the invite link you were sent (it looks like `…/band-invite/…`).
2. Create your account, or log in if you already have one.
3. Pick a username if asked. This is the name your bandmates see next to your notes and recordings.

If you were invited while you already had an account, the invite also shows up under **Profile → Invites**.

> **Tip:** You can belong to several bands. Each band has its own songs, setlists and gigs, and you switch between them from the band picker at the top of the sidebar.

## Finding your way around

![The band library: the sidebar on the left, the song list on the right.](library)

- **Sidebar** — everything in the current band: the **Library** (all songs), **Trash**, your **Songlists** and **Setlists**, **Technical Riders**, **Press Kits** and **Tours**. The small **+** next to each heading creates a new one. The bar under the band name shows how much storage the band has used.
- **Top bar** — the sidebar toggle, a light/dark/stage switch, fullscreen, the [user guide](/help) (this page) and your profile.
- **Round button in the corner** — the main "add" action for the page you are on: add a song in the Library, add songs in a setlist, add a gig on the Gigs page.

### On your phone

On a narrow screen the sidebar slides in from the left. Tap the sidebar button in the top bar, or swipe in from the left edge. Pick something and it closes again.

![The sidebar on a phone.](mobile-sidebar)

## Switching bands

Use the band name at the top of the sidebar to switch between bands. The **+** next to it creates a brand-new band, and you become its owner.

![The band switcher.](band-switcher)

## Light, dark and stage mode

The button in the top bar cycles through three looks: **light**, **dark**, and **stage mode**, a maximum-contrast look with an amber accent, made for reading a tablet under harsh or coloured stage lights. Gigboy remembers your choice on each device.

![A song in dark mode.](song-dark)

## Install it as an app

Gigboy is an installable web app. In Chrome, Edge or Safari, use **Install app** or **Add to Home Screen** and it opens like a normal app, full screen and with its own icon.
