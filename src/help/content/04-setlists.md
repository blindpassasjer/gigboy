---
title: Songlists and setlists
summary: Group songs into lists, build a setlist for a gig, and print it.
---

Gigboy has two kinds of list. They look similar but do different jobs:

- **Songlists** are free-form collections — "Celtic ballads", "Songs we're learning", "Christmas". A song can be in as many songlists as you like.
- **Setlists** are ordered lists for an actual gig. They show the running time, can carry a note per song, and can be played in [concert mode](/help/concert-mode).

Both live in the sidebar. Press the **+** next to **Songlists** or **Setlists** to create one.

## Building a setlist

![A setlist.](setlist)

- **Add songs** (the **+** at the top right) opens your library; pick the songs you want.
- **Reorder songs** (the up/down arrows) shows move-up and move-down buttons on every song. Press the button again when you are done.
- Each song shows its key, length and tempo, and the total running time is under the setlist name.
- Use the small note icon on a song to leave a **note for the stage** ("Open acoustic, no drums"). Notes appear on the printed sheet and at the top of the song in concert mode.
- The bin icon on a row removes the song from the setlist. The song stays in your library.

> **Tip:** You can also add a song to a list from the song itself: open it and press **Add to**, then tick the lists it should be in.

![The Add to menu on a song.](add-to-menu)

## Name and icon

Press the pencil next to the list name to rename it, or click the little icon to choose an emoji for the sidebar.

## Printing and PDF

![The print view.](setlist-print)

The printer button opens a print view with two layouts:

- **Setlist sheet** — one large-type page to tape to the floor: song order, key, BPM and your notes.
- **Full charts** — every song's full chart, one song per page.

Press **Print / Save as PDF** to print it or save a PDF.

## Import

The upload button on a list imports a list from a file, which is handy for moving a list between bands or restoring a backup.

## Playing it live

The green **play** button at the top of a setlist starts [concert mode](/help/concert-mode) on it.
