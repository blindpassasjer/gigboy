Promoters and sound engineers always ask for the same things. Build them once in Gigboy and send a link.

## Press kits

Press kits are in the sidebar under **Press Kits**. Press **+** to make one.

![A press kit.](press-kit)

- **Text** — write your band bio and description, with simple formatting: bold, italic, headings and lists.
- **Images** — drag photos in, tick the ones to include and drag them into the order you want. Your **band logo** is used too.
- **Tour dates** — tick the upcoming gigs to list. Ticking a tour lists all its gigs; open it to pick individual ones. Only confirmed, upcoming gigs show, with just the title, date and venue.

Make as many kits as you need — a short venue one-sheet and a longer festival kit, say.

### Sharing

Press **Share** to get a public link. Anyone with the link can read the kit without an account, and it previews nicely when pasted into an email or a chat. The download button saves the whole kit as a ZIP.

## Technical riders

Technical riders are in the sidebar under **Technical Riders**. A rider holds your **input list** (what goes into the desk) and your **stage plot**.

### Stage plot

![The stage plot editor.](stage-plot)

Press an instrument, a drum-kit piece or an amp to put it on the stage, then drag it into place. The numbered markers match your input list. Use **+ Custom** for anything that isn't listed.

### Sharing a rider

Like a press kit, press **Share** for a public link you can send to the venue, or download the rider as a ZIP.

> **Tip:** Attach the right rider and press kit to each gig on the [Gigs page](/help/bands-and-gigs) so everything for the show is in one place.
