## Your profile

Open **Profile** from the top bar.

![Your profile and account settings.](profile)

- Change your **name**, **username** and **email**.
- Change your **password** (you need your current one).
- **Log out** from the top of the page.
- Accept pending **band invites** under **Invites**.

## Storage

The bar under the band name in the sidebar shows how much storage the band has used. Click it for a breakdown of **recordings**, **images** and **attachments**. If you are running low, delete recordings or attachments you no longer need. The limit is set by whoever runs your Gigboy.

## Working offline

Gigboy is an installable app (see [Getting started](/help/getting-started)) and is built to keep working when you lose your connection, so it is a good idea to open tonight's setlist while you still have signal.

## Your data is yours

Under **Export your data** on your profile, press **Export as ZIP** to download everything: every song as a plain ChordPro file, plus recordings, press kit images, riders and gigs from all your bands. You can do this at any time, and the files open in any ChordPro-compatible app.

## Deleting things

Deleted songs, lists and press kits go to the **Trash** and can be restored for 30 days. Deleting your **account** (under **Danger zone** on your profile) permanently removes the bands you own and your profile data, so export first if you want to keep anything.
