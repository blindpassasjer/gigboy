## Your band

Everything in Gigboy belongs to a **band**: the songs, lists, riders, press kits and gigs. When you create a band you become its **owner**.

![Band settings.](band-settings)

The band owner opens **Band settings** with the cog at the top of the Library. There you can:

- change the **band name** and write a short **bio**;
- upload a **band logo**, which appears on the press kit and public pages;
- see the **members**;
- **create an invite link** to share with your bandmates.

### Inviting people

Press **Create invite link**, then copy it or share it straight from your phone. Anyone who opens it can create an account (or log in) and joins the band. One link works for as many bandmates as you like.

### Removing a member

The owner can remove a member with the **Remove** button next to their name. Deleting the whole band is at the very bottom, under **Danger zone**, and removes everything in it.

## Gigs and tours

Under **Tours** in the sidebar, **All gigs** shows every show. Pick a tour to see just its gigs.

![The gig calendar.](gigs-calendar)

Switch between **Calendar**, **Cards** and **List** views, and search by title, venue, contact or notes.

![Gigs as cards.](gigs-cards)

For each gig you can keep:

- the **date**, **get-in**, **sound-check** and **on-stage** times;
- the **venue** and address;
- a **contact person** with phone and email;
- notes ("Park in the back alley. Two sets of 45 minutes");
- the **setlist**, **technical rider** and **press kit** for that gig.

Press the round button on the Gigs page to add a gig, and group gigs into a **tour** with the **+** next to **Tours** in the sidebar. Old gigs move into an archive on their own.

### Subscribe in your calendar

Press **RSS feed** on the Gigs page to get your private calendar link. Add it to Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook and the band's gigs — with the schedule and the contact — show up there and stay up to date. The link is private to you, so don't share it.
